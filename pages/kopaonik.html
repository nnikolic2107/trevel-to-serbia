Welcome to Kopaonik: The "Mountain of the Sun"

Kopaonik is Serbia's premier mountain destination and the largest mountain range in the country, it is a protected National Park renowned for its stunning biodiversity and jagged, scenic landscapes. The mountain's highest point, Pančić’s Peak (Pančićev vrh), stands at an impressive 2,017 meters above sea level, offering a panoramic "roof of Serbia" view that stretches across the Balkans. Kopaonik is a true year-round destination, famously nicknamed the "Mountain of the Sun" because it enjoys over 200 sunny days annually. It is located in the central-southern part of Serbia, approximately 290 km south of Belgrade and 120 km from Niš. The mountain is easily accessible by car, with well-maintained roads leading to the resort area. For those relying on public transportation, regular bus services connect Kopaonik to major cities, making it a convenient destination for both domestic and international travelers.

Best Time to Visit Kopaonik

Kopaonik is a true year-round destination, famously nicknamed the "Mountain of the Sun" because it enjoys over 200 sunny days annually.

  • Winter (December to April): This is the peak season for snow lovers. Kopaonik transforms into a world-class ski resort with a reliable snow cover that often lasts 160 days a year. As the largest ski center in Serbia, it features over 62 km of slopes for alpine and Nordic skiing. There are trails for all levels, a snowboard park, and even night skiing on illuminated runs like Malo Jezero.
  • Spring and Summer (April to September): When the snow melts, Kopaonik reveals a lush green paradise. The mountain is blanketed in wildflowers and dense forests, making it a haven for hikers, mountain bikers, and nature lovers There are over 200 km of marked trails that wind through the park's diverse ecosystems, leading to hidden waterfalls, panoramic viewpoints, and tranquil alpine meadows. The cooler summer temperatures provide a refreshing escape from the heat of the lowlands, and the clear mountain air is perfect for stargazing at night.

Spas and Thermal Baths on Kopaonik, Serbia

Beyond the slopes and trails, Kopaonik is a sanctuary for relaxation. The mountain is home to some of the most luxurious wellness and spa centers in the Balkans, where guests can enjoy heated indoor-outdoor pools, salt rooms, and saunas with panoramic views of the pine forests. There are also natural hot springs located just a short drive from the tourist center. These use mineral-rich volcanic waters known for their healing properties.

  • Jošanička Banja: Located at the gateway to Kopaonik, this spa has some of the hottest thermal waters in Serbia (up to 77°C). It was recently renovated and features an ancient Turkish bath (hammam) and modern therapy pools. It's perfect for treating joints and muscles after an active day.
  • Lukovska Banja: Perched at 681 meters, this is the highest spa in Serbia and a true mountain gem. It is famous for its 37 thermal springs and the unique ability to swim in outdoor pools even while surrounded by deep snow. Visitors can enjoy "health trails" through the pine forests or relax in natural stone pools along the river. It is the perfect destination for those seeking a mix of high-altitude air and healing mineral waters.
  • Kuršumlijska Banja: Kuršumlijska Banja is one of Serbia's most significant and historically rich spa resorts, known for having some of the hottest thermal springs in the country, with temperatures reaching up to 64°C. Located in the Banjska river valley near Kuršumlija, the spa has a legacy of healing that dates back to Roman times
