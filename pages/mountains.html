Mountains of Serbia

Whether you are seeking the adrenaline of a downhill slope or the serenity of a sun-drenched meadow, Serbia’s mountains offer an unforgettable escape in every season. In winter, world-class resorts like Kopaonik and Stara Planina transform into snowy wonderlands perfect for skiing and snowboarding. As the snow melts, spring and summer reveal a lush hiker’s paradise, where you can explore the dense evergreen forests of Tara National Park or take in the golden vistas of Zlatibor. From high-altitude adventures and paragliding to peaceful walks by emerald lakes and hidden waterfalls, our mountains provide the perfect backdrop for your next great Serbian journey, no matter when you choose to visit.
